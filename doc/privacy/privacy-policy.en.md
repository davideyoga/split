# Split Privacy Policy

Last updated: [PUBLICATION DATE]

Split is an app for sharing expenses with other people and keeping track of your own. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, who can see it and what your rights are, as required by Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

**In short**

- We use your data only to run the app and keep it secure.
- There are no ads and no analytics or profiling tools, and we do not sell your data.
- Your email is not shown to other users. However, someone who knows it can find you and add you to an expense.
- People you share an expense or a group with can see your nickname and the details of that expense or group.
- You can ask for a copy of your data at any time and delete your account directly from your Profile.

## 2. What data we process

| Category | Data |
|---|---|
| **Account** | Email, nickname, a random internal identifier, account creation date, email confirmation |
| **Sign-in with an emailed code** | The sign-in code, stored only as a non-reversible hash |
| **Sign-in with Google or Apple** (where the button is available, and only if you choose to use it) | Your account identifier at the provider, email, name, profile picture if there is one, and the sign-in token signed by the provider. The name is used only to suggest a nickname, which you can change. The picture is stored but is not used or shown in the app |
| **Sessions** | Session token, IP address, browser and device type (user agent), creation and expiry dates |
| **Expenses** | Amount, currency, date, description, category, who paid, each participant's share, group if any, who created the expense and when |
| **Groups** | Group name, members, joining date, the invite link used to join (we store only a hash of the link, not the link itself) |
| **Repayments** | Who paid whom, amount, date, optional note |
| **Custom categories** | Name and appearance of the categories you create |
| **Notifications** | What happened, who did it, read status. For an expense that was deleted, or that you were removed from, we keep its description, amount, currency and category in order to show you the notification |
| **People you blocked** | The list of users you have blocked |
| **Technical data** | IP address, date and time of requests and any errors, in the hosting provider's technical logs. A failure to send a sign-in code may also log your email |

Payment details are never requested: Split records expenses but does not move money.

**Free text fields.** Descriptions, notes, and group and category names contain what you write, and other people can see them if the expense or group is shared. Please do not enter sensitive data (for example about health) or other people's data that is not needed.

## 3. Data about you entered by others

Not all data comes from you.

- A user who knows your nickname or email can add you to an expense and set its amount, share and description. The expense counts immediately and you get a notification in the app.
- A user can record a repayment between the two of you.
- During the test phase, some accounts were created by the administrator using the email and nickname provided at the time of the invitation.

If you do not want someone to add you, you can block them from the details of an expense you share. If you think some data is wrong, you can edit the expense if you take part in it, or write to us.

| Purpose | Legal basis (Art. 6 GDPR) |

| Creating and managing your account, letting you sign in, sending you the sign-in code | Performance of the service you requested (para. 1(b)) |
| Sign-in with Google or Apple, if you choose it | Performance of the service you requested (para. 1(b)) |
| Recording expenses, groups and repayments, calculating balances and your spending summary, sending you in-app notifications | Performance of the service you requested (para. 1(b)) |

Email and nickname are required: an account cannot be created without them. Other data depends on how you use the app.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on you. Balances and summaries are plain calculations on the recorded expenses.

## 5. Who can see your data

**Other Split users**

- **Expenses that are yours alone**, with no other participants and no group, are not visible to other users.
- People who **take part in an expense** with you can see your nickname, your share and all the details of the expense. They can also edit or delete it.
- **Members of a group** can see your nickname and all the group's expenses, including those from before they joined.
- A **repayment** is visible only to the two people involved.
- Someone who knows your **exact nickname or exact email** can find you in the search and see your nickname. Searching by your email therefore reveals that you have an account. Searches are limited in number, and a user you have blocked cannot find you.
- Anyone who receives a **group invite link** can see, even without an account, the group's name, the nickname of whoever created the link, the number of members and the link's expiry.

**Those who run Split.** The administrator can access data when needed for maintenance, security, backups or to respond to your requests.

**Providers processing data on our behalf** (processors, Art. 28 GDPR)

| Provider | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Render Services, Inc. (USA) | Hosting of the backend and the website | Backend in Frankfurt (EU). The website is delivered through the provider's global network |
| Neon, part of the Databricks group (USA) | Database | Frankfurt region (EU) |
| Brevo, Sendinblue SAS (France) | Sending the emails with the sign-in code. It receives your email and the content of the message | European Union |

**Google and Apple.** If you use their sign-in button, Google Ireland Limited or Apple Distribution International Ltd. process your data as independent controllers, under their own policies ([Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)). Where the Google button is available, it is loaded directly from Google's servers when you open the sign-in page. Google therefore receives your IP address and can read its own cookies, even if you do not press the button.

We do not share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

## 6. Transfers outside the European Union

We store data on servers located in the European Union. However, Render and Neon are US companies or controlled by US companies, so access from the United States may occur, for example for technical support or through their sub-processors.

These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified (Art. 45 GDPR), or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). You can ask us for more information at the address in section 1.

## 7. How long we keep data

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account, expenses, groups, repayments, categories, notifications, blocked people | As long as the account exists, unless you or another participant delete them earlier |
| Sign-in code | Valid for 10 minutes, then removed at the next automatic cleanup |
| Session | Expires 30 days after last use and is removed when you sign out. An expired session on a device no longer in use may stay stored, with its IP address and user agent, until the account is deleted |
| Link with Google or Apple | As long as the account exists |
| Hosting technical logs | 7 days on the current plan |
| Email sending logs | For the period set by Brevo |
| Database backups | The copies we make are deleted within 30 days and do not contain sessions or sign-in codes. The database provider also keeps a short technical history |

**If you delete your account from your Profile**, we delete or anonymise your data immediately (within 30 days if you ask by email): email, nickname, sessions, links with Google or Apple, expenses that are yours alone, custom categories, notifications, blocked people and the repayment notes you wrote. You also leave all your groups, and the invite links you created stop working.

Expenses and repayments shared with others are needed to calculate their balances, so they remain: your name is replaced with an anonymous label ("Deleted user"), even if you still have open balances. Shared expenses lose the custom categories you created. Text you wrote in a shared expense stays visible to the other participants, but you can edit it before deleting your account.

## 8. Data stored on your device

Split does not use profiling cookies or cookies of its own to run the app. It stores on your device only the strictly necessary information below, for which consent is not required:

| Name | Content | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `split_auth` (localStorage) | Session token, nickname, email and identifier | Until you sign out |
| `split.lang` (localStorage) | The language you chose | Until you clear your browser data |
| `split.monthStartDay` (localStorage) | The day your spending month starts | Until you clear your browser data |
| `split.inviteToken` (sessionStorage) | The invite link being used | Until the tab is closed |

If you install the app, your browser also keeps a copy of the application files so that it opens faster. This copy does not contain your expense data.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

- access your data and receive a copy of it;
- correct it (you can change your nickname directly from your Profile);
- delete it;
- restrict its processing;
- receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format (portability);
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise these rights, write to the address in section 1 from your account's email. We will reply free of charge within one month. You can delete your account directly from your Profile.

If you believe the processing breaks the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live or work.

## 10. Children

Split is not intended for people under 14. If you believe a child under 14 has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If the data processed, the providers or the purposes change, we will update this page and the date at the top. We will let you know in the app about important changes.
